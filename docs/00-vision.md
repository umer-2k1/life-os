# 00 · Vision

## The one sentence
Remind me every day of the life I'm building, keep me emotionally obsessed with it, and make a wasted day impossible to ignore — so my dreams take **months, not years**.

## The real problem (name it honestly)
- I am ambitious but I **procrastinate**. Goals that should take months take me 3–5 years.
- I don't lack dreams. I lack a system that makes **inaction loud** and **progress addictive**.
- A dashboard I have to *remember to open* loses to procrastination every time.

## The core bet
> **The bot is the product. The website is the trophy room.**

The system must **reach out**, not wait. Daily. In my face. The website is where I go to *feel* the progress and get the dopamine; the real behavior change happens in the daily loop the bot runs.

## Design principles (non-negotiable)

1. **Loss > gain.** Humans move harder to avoid loss than to chase reward. So: breakable streaks, dreams that visibly **decay in red** when neglected, a timeline that *slips* when I waste time.
2. **Price the waste.** "3h YouTube" isn't a red box — it's **"≈ +2 days added to your timeline to the Dream Car."** Convert wasted time into *days stolen from my dreams*. That is what creates the sleepless feeling — not decoration.
3. **Shrink every dream to ONE next action.** Vision is abstract; momentum is concrete. The AI's job is always to answer: *"the single thing this week that moves this dream."*
4. **One brutal daily number (0–100).** Did today move money/goals — yes or no. It feeds the heatmap. No hiding.
5. **Emotion is a feature, not a note.** Every dream carries my raw *why* — a letter to myself, the feeling, the reason it hurts. The system **resurfaces it on purpose** to re-trigger the spark.
6. **Survive my laziness.** The whole thing must work on **one voice note a day, max**. Everything else is AI-derived. If it needs more effort than that, it fails.
7. **No fluff, ever.** Bullet points and keywords. Never 5-line paragraphs. If I see a wall of text, I won't read it.
8. **Relevance is judged, not assumed.** "Reached out to 5 recruiters" = on-track (green). "Watched GTA 6 streams" = off-track (red). The AI scores each activity against *my* goals.

## The emotional layer
- A permanent, visible line: **"Make a shit ton of money. Nobody cares about it."**
- A **North Star number** (target income / net worth) pinned everywhere — every dream's money ladders up to it.
- An **identity line** under it: *who I'm becoming* ("I'm someone who closes clients"), because obsession is about identity, not tasks.
- Big photographs. The feeling behind the car, not just the car.

## What "done well" feels like
- I open it and feel **excited and slightly anxious** — in a good way.
- A bad day is **uncomfortable to log** because I can see the cost.
- A good streak is **addictive**.
- I start **dreaming about the goals** because the system keeps them in front of me.

## Honest limit
A tool can **spark** discipline; it can't **be** it. What this system *can* do: make a bad day impossible to ignore, make a good streak addictive, and keep the dream emotionally alive. Everything here is designed around that — nothing pretends to be magic.
